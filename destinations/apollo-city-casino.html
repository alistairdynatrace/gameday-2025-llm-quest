Destination Apollo City Casino

Apollo City Casino is a family-friendly gaming destination on the Moon's Oceanus Procellarum. It has a bustling population of 4,200 residents including many casino workers.

The Aldrin International Spaceport connects to Apollo City via the famous Lunar Express monorail system that offers stunning views of the lunar landscape.

Apollo City Casino features beginner-friendly games, anti-gravity bowling, and the popular Lunar Lottery. The casino is known for its oxygen gardens and the weekly meteor shower viewing parties from the observation deck.
