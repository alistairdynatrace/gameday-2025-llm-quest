Destination Dark Side Palace

Dark Side Palace is a mysterious underground casino complex on the Moon's hidden hemisphere. It has a secretive population of 892 residents who rarely venture to the surface.

The Chang'e Secret Landing Bay is accessible only to invited guests, with underground tunnels leading directly to the casino's main floor via silent electric carts.

Dark Side Palace offers exclusive shadow poker, quantum dice games, and the infamous Black Hole Slots. The casino operates in perpetual darkness with bioluminescent decorations and is rumored to never close.
