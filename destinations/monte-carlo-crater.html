Destination Monte Carlo Crater

Monte Carlo Crater is an exclusive lunar casino resort built inside a massive impact crater on the Moon's far side. It has a select population of 156 high-roller residents.

The Gagarin Private Terminal serves only VIP spacecraft, with luxury rovers providing transport down the crater walls to the casino floor.

Monte Carlo Crater specializes in high-stakes baccarat and lunar blackjack. The casino is famous for its Earth-view dining and the legendary Moonstone Jackpot that hasn't been won in 47 years.
