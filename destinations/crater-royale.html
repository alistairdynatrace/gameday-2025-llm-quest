Destination Crater Royale

Crater Royale is a mid-tier casino resort built on the rim of Tycho Crater on the Moon's southern highlands. It has a diverse population of 1,650 residents from across the solar system.

The Shepard Regional Spaceport offers budget-friendly transport options, with solar-powered buses climbing the crater rim roads to reach the casino complex.

Crater Royale features classic Earth-style games with a lunar twist, including low-gravity craps and magnetic card tables. The casino is famous for its crater-rim restaurant and the annual Earthrise Tournament that attracts players from Mars and beyond.
