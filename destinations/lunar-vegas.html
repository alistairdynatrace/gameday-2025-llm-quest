Destination Lunar Vegas

Lunar Vegas is a dazzling casino city on the Moon's Sea of Tranquility. It has a population of 2,847 residents and millions of annual visitors.

The Armstrong Spaceport is the main gateway to Lunar Vegas, with regular shuttle services to the casino district via magnetic levitation pods.

Lunar Vegas features zero-gravity poker tables, holographic slot machines, and the famous Crater Roulette where the ball floats in low gravity. The neon lights are visible from Earth on clear nights.
